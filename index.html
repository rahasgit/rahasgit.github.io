About Me

I’m a doctoral candidate in Intelligent Systems at Bielefeld University, supervised by Hendrik Buschmeier, and a research associate in the project Monitoring The Understanding of Explanations.

Topics of Interest

The project: In the course of a conversation, the listener signals their non/understanding by providing multimodal cues (such as a head nod or a “hmm”), which the speaker relies on to know whether they can go on with their current explanation, or whether they should clarify, rephrase or repeat something.

In my research, I investigate to what extent the speaker’s sensitivity to such signals can/should be computationally modeled in a way that an AI agent can show a similar adaptivity in interactions.

More generally, I am interested in pragmatic abilities of language models, and am curious about model training ways other than data/parameter scaling, e.g. cognitively-inspired approaches.

Outside of work, I watch a lot of movies, and some of my favorites are Scandinavian dark comedy and Korean horror movies. When I read books, it is usually dystopian science fiction. I like Depeche Mode, portrait photography, east Asian cuisine and Sims. I’m bad at, but enjoy swimming.
