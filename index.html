About Me

I’m a first year doctoral candidate in Intelligent Systems at Bielefeld University, supervised by Hendrik Buschmeier. My role is integrated in TRR 318: Co-constructing Explainability, and I am a research associate in one of its projects, Monitoring The Understanding of Explanations.

Prior to this, I did a research internship in computational linguistics with Sina Zarriess, and finished my Master’s degree in Language Technologies.

Topics of Interest

In the course of a conversation, the listener signals their non/understanding by providing multimodal cues (such as a head nod or a “hmm”), which the speaker relies on to know whether they can go on with their current explanation, or whether they should clarify, rephrase or repeat something.

In my research, I investigate to what extent the speaker’s sensitivity to such signals can/should be computationally modeled in a way that an AI agent can show a similar adaptivity in interactions.

I am also interested in pragmatic abilities of language models, and am curious about model training ways other than data/parameter scaling, e.g. cognitively-inspired approaches.
